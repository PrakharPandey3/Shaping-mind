02 My Journey

A career built around meaningful education.

Roli's journey in education has been driven by one central question:

How can we make education more meaningful, relevant, and transformative for every learner?

Over the years, she has worked across academics, administration, teacher training, curriculum planning, assessment, school improvement, parent engagement, student discipline, admissions, and institutional development.

Her leadership experience has involved building academic systems, mentoring educators, strengthening classroom practices, monitoring learning outcomes, and creating processes that bring greater consistency and accountability to school functioning.

She believes that education is not merely the transmission of knowledge — it is the formation of character, confidence, competence, and conscience.

“When teachers grow, classrooms transform. When classrooms transform, children flourish.”

06 My Vision
“To create responsible, confident, ethical, and innovative learners who contribute positively to society and lead the nation with knowledge, compassion, and character.”

Roli envisions schools where children do not learn merely to pass examinations.

They learn to understand. To question. To create. To connect. To contribute.

She advocates an education system where conceptual clarity replaces rote learning, curiosity replaces fear, and character stands alongside achievement.

A Teacher at Heart.
A Leader by Responsibility.

Although her professional roles have evolved into leadership, Roli continues to identify herself fundamentally as an educator.

For her, leadership is not about a designation or a cabin. It is about creating direction, developing people, solving problems, building systems, and leaving an institution stronger than it was before.

She believes an effective educational leader must be able to see the school from multiple perspectives: through the eyes of a child, the expectations of a parent, the challenges of a teacher, and the responsibilities of management.

This ability to connect these perspectives shapes her leadership approach.
